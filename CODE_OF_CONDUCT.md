# Code of Conduct

Be direct, be honest, review the work and not the person. No harassment,
no personal attacks, no silent sabotage of other people's work.

Maintainers enforce this. Reports go privately to the maintainers, who
respond within seven days. Violations mean a warning, then a temporary
ban, then a permanent ban.
